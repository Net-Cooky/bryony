05 · The Making Process

One dress. Six steps. Made entirely around you.

We generally recommend beginning 6 to 9 months before your wedding date. Custom-made bridal gowns take time and care, from your initial consultation through to the final fittings. Starting comfortably early takes the pressure out of it.

01 of 06 Say hello
  1. 01

    Say hello

    Any time

    You are welcome to contact us for a complimentary, no-obligation appointment. Tell us roughly when you are getting married and what you picture. We are flexible to your schedule, with evening appointments available if you need them.

  2. 02

    The first visit

    To begin

    During your first visit we discuss our services, ideas and pricing, and suggest how to achieve your vision. Bring photographs, drawings, pinned images, a favourite colour: anything that shows what you are naturally drawn to.

  3. 03

    Design, fabric & plan

    6–9 months out

    We take the time to discuss what fabrics, style, lines and silhouette you want to achieve, and how the dress will fit and suit your entire wedding day: the venue, the weather, and dancing into the night. Then fabrics are selected and pattern making begins.

  4. 04

    First fitting

    2–6 months out

    Come with your wedding shoes and chosen undergarments along with you, as they change how a gown sits and falls. This is the fitting to assess drape, movement and silhouette, and to communicate any adjustments you would like.

  5. 05

    Second fitting

    About 1 month out

    Adjustments from the first fitting are refined. You should be able to move freely and comfortably in your gown by now. Bring the accessories or veil you plan to wear to see the complete look come together.

  6. 06

    Final fitting & handover

    Before the wedding

    An exciting and practical day. We ensure you understand how the dress works: the zip, delicate buttons, bustling and corset support, so bring whoever will be helping you dress on the day. We discuss gown care, then you take your dress home.

07 · The Dressmaker

A decade of one dress at a time.

Bryony Jayne Reardon has worked in the Melbourne bridal industry independently as a dressmaker for over ten years, and previously at both Helen Brownhill in Malvern and Raffaele Ciuca in Armadale, before establishing her own bridal label, Bryony Jayne Bridal Couture, in early 2013.

Prior to working independently, Bryony received a Bachelor of Fashion from Box Hill Institute, and emerged twice in a row as a finalist in Gown of the Year: the creative exhibition show organised and run by the late, iconic Guy Barton, held annually at the Crown Ballroom.
