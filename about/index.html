Our Experience & Story

Couture craft, quiet attention.

Bryony Jayne Reardon, owner and dressmaker of Bryony Jayne Bridal Couture, has worked in the Melbourne bridal industry independently as a dressmaker for over ten years, and previously at both Helen Brownhill in Malvern and Raffaele Ciuca in Armadale, before establishing her own independent bridal brand in early 2013.

Prior to working independently, Bryony received a Bachelor of Fashion from Box Hill Institute and emerged twice in a row as a finalist in the creative exhibition show Gown of the Year: organised and run by the late, iconic Guy Barton at the Crown Ballroom.

In late 2019, New Idea Magazine honoured our label by naming our Winter gown as one of the top five plus-size dresses in Melbourne, writing that it was "one of the best beach wedding dresses we've seen."

The Philosophy

A Calm, Personal Experience

Bridal dress shopping should not feel rushed, overwhelming, or pressured by sales targets. In our private Berwick atelier, we book one bride at a time. The studio, the sample gowns, and Bryony's dedicated attention are exclusively yours during your visit.

Bryony loves working with brides across every silhouette: from petite to fuller figures. Instead of trying to squeeze into a standard dress size from an overseas factory, your gown is patterned specifically to fit your unique proportions, posture and personal style.

“My goal is for every bride to feel completely herself: confident, comfortable, and beautifully radiant in a dress made entirely for her.”

Transparent & Thoughtful Pricing

We believe in complete transparency. During your complimentary consultation, we discuss fabrics, complexity and budget together before cutting any cloth or asking for a commitment. Whether creating a custom gown from scratch or adapting a signature studio piece, we respect your budget and time throughout the entire process.
