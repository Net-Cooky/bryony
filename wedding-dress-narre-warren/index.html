The Six Steps

One dress. Six steps. Made entirely around you.

We generally recommend beginning 6 to 9 months before your wedding. Every dress evolves through dedicated fittings in our Berwick studio.

From initial sketches to final hand-stitched lace, Bryony guides you through every decision with honesty, warmth, and care.

Book your consultation
  1. 01

    First conversation & sketch

    Step 01

    An obligation-free meeting at our Berwick studio to discuss silhouettes, lace swatches, venue details, and preferred budget.

  2. 02

    Measurement & pattern draft

    Step 02

    Bryony takes comprehensive body measurements and drafts an original pattern tailored specifically to your posture and proportions.

  3. 03

    The calico toile fitting

    Step 03

    A mock-up gown sewn in plain cotton calico is fitted to your body. We sculpt the neckline, hem, and waistline until the balance is effortless.

  4. 04

    Cutting into fine silk

    Step 04

    Once the toile is approved, your actual fabrics: pure silks, French Chantilly lace, and lightweight linings are cut and hand-sewn.

  5. 05

    Silk & lace fittings

    Step 05

    Try on your gown in its real materials. Bryony adjusts bustier boning, ensures seam precision, and pins lace appliques by hand.

  6. 06

    Final collection

    Step 06

    Your finished bespoke wedding dress is pressed, inspected, and placed into a protective bridal garment bag ready for your wedding day.
