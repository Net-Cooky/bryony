Personalisation

How to Adapt a Signature Gown

Our signature gowns are designed to be starting points for your imagination, not rigid moulds.

Option 01

Neckline & Straps

Loved the skirt on Winter but prefer a sweetheart or square neckline? We can adapt the neckline, alter strap widths, or add delicate off-the-shoulder straps.

Option 02

Sleeves & Cuffs

Add detachable lace sleeves for your ceremony that slip off for evening dancing, or incorporate soft bishop sleeves in sheer silk organza.

Option 03

Trains & Bustling

Every gown's train can be extended to chapel length, made dramatic with scalloped lace, or kept neat and sweep-only for outdoor garden weddings.

Option 04

Silks & Colours

All designs can be created in shades ranging from crisp ivory to warm champagne, blush or almond, using pure silk crepe, satin or chiffon.
