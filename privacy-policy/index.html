Legal & Privacy

Privacy Policy

Bryony Jayne Bridal Couture is committed to protecting the privacy of our website visitors and clients in accordance with the Australian Privacy Principles.

Information We Collect

Visitors can browse the general areas of our website without providing personal details. When you make an appointment enquiry or contact us directly, we collect information you provide voluntarily: such as your name, email address, phone number, approximate wedding date, and design preferences.

How We Use Your Information

Any personal details you provide are used strictly to communicate with you about your bridal appointment, answer your questions, and arrange fittings. We do not sell, rent, lease or disclose your contact information to external marketers or third parties.

Data Security

Our website utilizes industry-standard SSL (Secure Sockets Layer) encryption to secure the transmission of data between your browser and our server.

Cookies & Analytics

We may use basic cookies or privacy-conscious analytics tools to understand website traffic patterns, popular articles, and user experience. These tools do not record personally identifying information.

Contacting Us About Your Privacy

If you have any questions about this privacy statement or wish to update or delete any details you have submitted, please contact us directly:
