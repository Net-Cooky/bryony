Steps to Finding the Perfect Wedding Dress

Bridal Guide ◆ By Bryony Jayne Reardon ◆ Berwick Studio

Now that the countdown to your wedding day has begun, finding your wedding dress is one of the most personal experiences of the journey. Dress shopping doesn't have to feel overwhelming or high-pressure. With thoughtful preparation and a clear perspective, it becomes an inspiring and relaxed creative process. Here are five practical steps to guide you toward a gown that feels completely your own.

1. Start Planning Early (6 to 9 Months)

Fine bridal gowns require dedicated craftsmanship: pattern drafting, calico mock-ups, hand-cutting, and detailed finishing. We recommend allowing 6 to 9 months between your first visit and your wedding date. Starting early ensures an unhurried, enjoyable rhythm throughout your wedding dress fitting process, leaving plenty of breathing room in the final weeks before your wedding.

2. Clarify Your Silhouette & Aesthetic

Before stepping into a boutique or atelier, spend time noticing what you naturally gravitate toward in everyday clothing. Do you love high necklines, open backs, structured waists, or fluid column skirts? Exploring different shapes in our wedding dress silhouettes guide helps narrow down styles that complement your posture and venue before you try on gowns.

3. Discuss Budget & Fabrics Openly

One of the most reassuring steps is establishing your comfortable investment range early. In an independent atelier, discussing pricing alongside fabric choices like silk crepe, French lace, or organza ensures complete transparency with zero hidden alteration fees down the line. Learn more about how textiles behave in our wedding dress fabrics guide.

4. Choose an Intimate, Trusted Entourage

While it is tempting to bring a large bridal party, having too many conflicting voices can distract from how you genuinely feel in the gown. Bring one or two trusted companions, such as your mother, sister, or closest friend, whose opinions feel supportive and honest. Ultimately, how you feel standing in front of the mirror is the only opinion that truly matters.

5. Consider Custom Bespoke vs Adapting a Sample

You don't need to choose between a cookie-cutter off-the-rack dress and starting entirely from scratch. At our Berwick atelier, brides can commission a completely bespoke wedding dress designed around their measurements, or try on and adapt one of our seven signature studio gowns by customising sleeves, back drops, or fabrics.

Written by Bryony Jayne Reardon

Founder and bridal couture dressmaker at Bryony Jayne Bridal Couture in Berwick, Melbourne. Helping Victorian brides create custom wedding dresses since 2013.
