What to Expect

Your Studio Visit

We want your appointment to be an enjoyable, pressure-free part of your wedding planning journey.

01 · Exclusivity

A Private Studio

We host one bride at a time. The entire studio, rail of gowns and Bryony's full attention are dedicated to you and your companions throughout your visit.

02 · Discussion

Collaborative Design

Bring your inspiration, photos, sketches or fabrics. We discuss your silhouette preferences, wedding venue, and how you want to move and feel on the day.

03 · Try On

Sample Gowns to Try

You can try on our seven signature studio gowns to see different necklines, waistlines, and fabric drapes on your figure.

04 · Clarity

Open Budget Advice

We discuss pricing, fabric choices and timelines openly before any commitment. No high-pressure sales tactics or hidden alteration fees.
